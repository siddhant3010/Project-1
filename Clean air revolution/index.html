About The Project

Clean Air Revolution is a smart platform that captures carbon waste data from industries, analyzes carbon quality using AI, and connects industries with recyclers and buyers to create high-value products from emissions.

The Problem

🌫 Air Pollution

Industrial emissions continue to increase harmful carbon particles in the atmosphere.

❤️ Health Risk

Millions suffer from respiratory and cardiovascular diseases due to poor air quality.

🌎 Climate Impact

Excess carbon accelerates global warming and environmental degradation.
